About Saint Mark

Who We Are

Saint Mark Missionary Baptist Church is a faith-filled church family committed to worship, discipleship, outreach, and serving our community.


Our mission is rooted in reaching the lost, making disciples, and transforming lives through faith, love, and service.

Our Mission

To serve God, strengthen families, and impact our community through worship, discipleship, outreach, and Christian fellowship.

Our Vision

Reaching the Lost, Making Disciples, and Transforming Lives.

Our Community

We are a church family built on faith, fellowship, service, and love for one another.
