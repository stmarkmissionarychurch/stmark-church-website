Rev. James E. Jordan Sr.

Pastor of Saint Mark Missionary Baptist Church

Pastor James Jordan

Rev. James Jordan is a family man who loves God and His people. His life’s mission is centered on loving the Lord with all his heart, soul, and mind, and loving his neighbor as himself.

Ministry Journey

Pastor Jordan received his License in Christian Ministry in January 2006 and his Ordination certificate in May 2010 from The Greater Solid Rock Baptist Church in Atlanta, Georgia.

Leadership & Service

He served in the U.S. Army, earned the rank of Sergeant, and later retired from the U.S. Postal Service after 23 years as a Logistics Manager.

First Lady Ava Felicia Jordan

First Lady Ava Felicia Jordan is passionate about ministry, encouragement, outreach, and helping people reach their God-given potential.

Education & Career

She earned her undergraduate degree from Talladega College in Economics and Business Administration, and later earned a Master’s degree in Organizational Psychology from Capella University.

Ministry & Outreach

As an ordained minister of Blessed Success Ministries, Inc., she conducts workshops, supports community outreach, and provides spiritual encouragement and life skills coaching.

Preaching

Sharing the Word of God with purpose, wisdom, and love.

Outreach

Serving the community and helping those in need.

Family

Leading with faith, family values, and compassion.

Discipleship

Encouraging others to grow spiritually and walk with God.

Pastor Jordan’s Favorite Quote

“Man’s love is always and only a response to God’s love.”
